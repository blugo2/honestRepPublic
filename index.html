Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 6, 2026

Introduction

HonestRep is an iOS app that uses your device's camera and Apple's on-device pose detection to count push-up repetitions and score your form in real time. HonestRep is operated by [YOUR NAME OR COMPANY].

This policy explains what information HonestRep stores, how it is used, and what it absolutely does not do. The short version: everything stays on your device, nothing is sent to us, and we do not know who you are.

Questions? Contact us at support@honestrep.app.

Data We Collect

HonestRep requires no account and creates no profile. All data the app generates is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData and standard system storage (UserDefaults). None of it is transmitted to us or any third party.

Workout & goal data (SwiftData — local only)

Your workout sessions, rep counts, form grades, daily goals, streaks, and lifetime statistics are stored in a local database on your device. Deleting the app permanently erases this data.

App preferences & settings (UserDefaults — local only)

The following values are stored as plain key-value pairs on your device:

What is stored Why
Onboarding completed flag To skip the intro screens after first launch
Preferred daily rep goal To remember your target between sessions
Voice guidance on/off To preserve your audio preference
Daily reminder on/off & time To schedule your local push notification
Pro entitlement status & trial flag Cached from StoreKit so the UI renders correctly on cold launch without waiting for an App Store network call
Subscription expiration date Cached from StoreKit for the same reason
Session count & paywall display flags To decide when to show the upgrade prompt

Subscription billing

Subscription purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. HonestRep never sees your payment information. Apple's handling of transaction data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Camera & Pose Detection

HonestRep requests access to your device's camera for one purpose only: detecting your body position during a push-up session.

✓ Camera frames are processed entirely on your device using Apple's Vision framework.

✓ No video, images, or pose data are ever recorded, saved beyond the active session, or sent anywhere.

✓ We have no access to your camera feed — it never leaves your device.

✓ When you end or pause a session, camera processing stops immediately.

What We Do Not Collect

HonestRep does not collect, store, or transmit any of the following:

Name or email address Location data Health or HealthKit records Payment information Device identifiers (IDFA, IDFV) Advertising IDs IP address Crash logs Analytics events Behavioral tracking data Video or images Pose keypoints Microphone audio

Third-Party Services

HonestRep has zero third-party SDKs. There are no analytics libraries, no crash reporters, no advertising networks, and no social login frameworks embedded in the app.

The only external service the app interacts with is the Apple App Store, via Apple's own StoreKit framework, for processing subscription purchases and verifying entitlements. Apple's data practices for this are covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Daily reminder notifications are delivered by iOS's local notification system. They are scheduled on-device and do not go through any third-party push notification service.

Data Retention & Deletion

All app data — workout history, goals, preferences, and settings — is stored on your device and under your control at all times.

To delete all your data: delete the HonestRep app from your device. iOS will remove the app's SwiftData database and all UserDefaults entries automatically. This action is irreversible.

If you have questions about data or want to make a deletion request, contact us at support@honestrep.app. Since we do not collect data on our servers, we cannot delete data that was never transmitted to us — but we are happy to help you understand what is stored locally and how to remove it.

Children's Privacy

HonestRep is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the app, please contact us at support@honestrep.app and we will take appropriate steps.
